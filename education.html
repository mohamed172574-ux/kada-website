Three habits

Simple habits that prevent most violations.

01

Know the substance

An everyday pharmacy product can be prohibited in competition. Check every medicine with the medicine checker or your team doctor — before you take it.

Open the medicine checker
02

Beware of supplements

A supplement can be contaminated with a prohibited substance not shown on the label. Strict liability means you are responsible for anything found in your sample — prefer batch-tested products and keep the receipt.

Read the supplements FAQ
03

Ask the agency

Not sure? A course, the AI assistant or a call to KADA — asking is free; a violation can cost a career.

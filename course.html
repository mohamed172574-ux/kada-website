Anti-Doping Essentials for athletes

Everything a national-level athlete must know before the season: the Prohibited List, the testing procedure, TUEs, your rights and responsibilities.

About this course

Anti-Doping Essentials is KADA's foundation course, mandatory for athletes entering a national team. It is delivered by certified KADA educators with real doping-control equipment and interactive case studies.

You will learn

  • The 11 anti-doping rule violations and strict liability
  • How to read the Prohibited List and use the medicine checker
  • A doping control, step by step — your rights and duties
  • Your rights in results management: B-sample analysis and a fair hearing
  • Therapeutic Use Exemptions: when and how to apply
  • Supplement risks and the Speak Up channel

Instructor

Dr. Dalal Al-Sabah — Head of Education, certified WADA educator, former national-team volleyball player.

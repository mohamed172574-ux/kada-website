Scope summary

KADA invites bids for the supply, delivery and storage of tamper-evident sample collection kits meeting the WADA International Standard for Testing and Investigations, including unique numbering compatible with KADA's digital Doping Control Form and chain-of-custody tracking.

  • Urine kits with A/B bottles, security seals and QR-coded labels
  • Blood kits for Athlete Biological Passport (ABP) and serum analysis, with temperature indicators
  • Quarterly deliveries to KADA's secure storage; emergency delivery within 72 hours
  • Kit-number data feed (API) to the KADA operations platform

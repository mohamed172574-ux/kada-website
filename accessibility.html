Accessibility statement

KADA is committed to a website and app that everyone can use — athletes, parents, officials and the public — regardless of ability, device or language. This site targets WCAG 2.2 level AA.

Try these display options on this page. Your choice is stored on this device only.

What we have done

  • Full Arabic (RTL) and English (LTR) versions of every page and every e-service, with a persistent language choice.
  • Semantic HTML landmarks, a logical heading order and descriptive labels for screen-reader users.
  • A “Skip to main content” link is the first item on every page — press Tab once to reveal it.
  • Visible keyboard focus on every link, button, tab, accordion and form field; all menus operable with Tab, Enter and Escape.
  • Colour contrast of at least 4.5 : 1 for text; status never conveyed by colour alone (badges include text).
  • Animations respect the operating-system "reduce motion" setting automatically.
  • Forms with clear labels, required-field markers, inline error messages and no time limits.
  • The AI assistant supports voice input (speech-to-text) in Arabic and English, with read-aloud available as an option.
  • Mobile app: dynamic type, VoiceOver / TalkBack labels and offline mode for field use.

Known limitations

Some older PDF publications are scanned images and not yet tagged for screen readers. We are re-publishing them as accessible PDFs and HTML; contact us to request an accessible copy of any document.

Standards and testing

The site is designed against WCAG 2.2 AA and applicable Kuwaiti government e-service guidelines. We test with NVDA, VoiceOver and keyboard-only navigation on each release, and review accessibility periodically.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier, email accessibility@kada.gov.kw or use the contact form. We aim to respond within five working days.

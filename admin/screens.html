What “Deep” means

A deep screen shows the complete workflow with every state, decision and side panel the staff member needs — the way it will work in production. Standard screens show the final layout and main actions; detailed sub-flows are specified at implementation.

Executive data dependency

Testing, results-management and committee figures in the Executive dashboard come from the KADA mobile application / operations systems. The website platform provides the dashboard, GIS layer and reporting; data feeds are part of the app scope.

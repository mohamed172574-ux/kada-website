Vision, mission and values

What drives the agency, and the principles every employee, inspector and committee member commits to.

Vision

A Kuwaiti sporting community where clean sport is the only sport.

To be a regional reference in anti-doping — trusted by athletes, respected by partners and recognised internationally for excellence.

Mission

Protect athletes' health and the integrity of competition.

Through intelligent testing, fair results management, continuous education and transparent public reporting, in full compliance with the World Anti-Doping Code.

Our values

Four values, one standard.

01Integrity

We apply the rules equally to every athlete, every federation and ourselves.

02Independence

Case decisions are taken by independent committees, free from sporting, commercial or political influence.

03Transparency

Sanctions, decisions and reports are published in line with the Code; processes are auditable end to end.

04Care

Athlete health and dignity come first — in testing, in hearings and in education.

Strategic objectives 2026–2030

Where we are heading.

Direct testing to where the risk of doping is highest, rather than simply increasing numbers: based on risk assessment for each sport, confidential reports received through Speak Up, and monitoring of athletes' biological markers through the Athlete Biological Passport.

Every national-team athlete and coach completes an anti-doping education course before their first competition season.

All public services online in Arabic and English; field operations fully paperless; AI assistants available 24/7.

Pass every WADA compliance review with no major findings, and implement each new International Standard from the day it takes effect, safeguarding Kuwait's standing in the international anti-doping system.

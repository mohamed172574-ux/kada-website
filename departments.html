Departments

Five departments deliver the agency's mandate, supported by the platform's role-based workspaces.

01

Doping Control & Operations

Designs the annual Test Distribution Plan, manages sample collection, and coordinates field inspectors through the mobile application with GPS check-in, digital Doping Control Forms and DocuSign signatures.

  • Risk-based test planning by sport and season
  • Inspector scheduling, assignment and live GPS monitoring
  • Sample chain of custody to WADA-accredited laboratories
  • Offline-first field operations with automatic sync
See the inspector & operations app

02

Results Management

Receives laboratory results, registers cases, performs the initial review, assigns cases to the competent committee and — as the single official channel — notifies athletes and tracks their responses and deadlines.

Business rule

All official communications with athletes are issued exclusively by this department. Committee members do not contact athletes outside formal hearings.

Intake
Laboratory report · Doping Control & Operations referral · Investigations referral for a non-analytical violation
Review
Initial review: check for an existing TUE and for any departure from testing or laboratory procedures
Assignment
Referral of cases to the Results Management and Disciplinary committees · referral of retroactive TUE requests to the TUE Committee
Notification
Official letter to the athlete with the finding and their rights (B sample) · provisional suspension where applicable · deadline clock starts
Tracking
Responses, hearings, decisions, closure & publication

The 2027 Prohibited List is now available in Arabic

01 · 10 · 2026KADA Media Center4 min read

The Kuwait Anti-Doping Agency has published the Arabic version of the World Anti-Doping Agency's 2027 Prohibited List, together with a simplified guide and an updated version of the KADA medicine checker. The list enters into force on 1 January 2027.

"Athletes should never have to guess," said the Chairman of KADA. "Publishing the list in Arabic, three months before it takes effect, with every change explained in plain language, is the practical side of our commitment to education before enforcement."

What changes in 2027

  • Tramadol remains prohibited in competition, with clarified washout guidance.
  • Two new SARMs and one metabolic modulator are added as examples under S1 and S4.
  • Glucocorticoid guidance updated with clearer washout periods by route of administration.
  • Monitoring Program expanded to include additional stimulant combinations.
The medicine checker now covers more than 2,400 brand-name products registered in Kuwait, each mapped to its active ingredients and WADA class.

Where to find it

The Arabic and English lists, the summary of modifications and the simplified guide are available on the Prohibited List & Medicine Checker page and in the Research & Publications library. Athletes using the KADA app will also receive a push notification.

Federations are asked to circulate the list to their medical staff and to register team doctors for the TUE & Prescribing for Athletes course on 2 December.

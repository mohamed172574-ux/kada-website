What is prohibited, and when

A plain-language guide. Tap a filter, then a class to expand. The official list is linked on the right.

Any pharmacological substance not approved for human therapeutic use by any government health authority — e.g. drugs in pre-clinical or clinical development, designer drugs, veterinary medicines.

Anabolic androgenic steroids (testosterone, nandrolone, stanozolol…) and other anabolic agents such as clenbuterol and SARMs.

EPO and agents affecting erythropoiesis, growth hormone and its releasing factors, IGF-1 and other growth factors.

All beta-2 agonists are prohibited except inhaled salbutamol, formoterol, salmeterol and vilanterol within specified doses.

Aromatase inhibitors, anti-estrogens, myostatin inhibitors, insulins and metabolic modulators such as meldonium.

Furosemide, hydrochlorothiazide, desmopressin, probenecid and plasma expanders.

Amphetamines, cocaine, methylphenidate, pseudoephedrine (above threshold) and many cold & flu ingredients. Prohibited in competition only.

Morphine, fentanyl, tramadol; natural and synthetic cannabinoids; all injectable, oral and rectal glucocorticoids — in competition.

Prohibited in particular sports such as archery and shooting (at all times) and in competition in others.

Blood manipulation; chemical and physical manipulation, including sample tampering and IV infusions above 100 mL per 12 hours; gene and cell doping.

Supplements guidance

Supplements may contain prohibited substances not listed on the label, and the athlete is responsible for everything that enters their body. Batch-tested products (e.g. Informed Sport, NSF Certified for Sport) reduce but do not remove the risk. Keep receipts and lot numbers.

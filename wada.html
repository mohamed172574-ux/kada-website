Official resources

Three official platforms, one click away.

WADA portal

The World Anti-Doping Code, International Standards, Prohibited List, compliance programme and news from the global regulator.

Open wada-ama.org

ADEL — Anti-Doping Education & Learning

Free certified e-learning for athletes, coaches, doctors and parents. KADA recognises ADEL certificates in its education programme.

Open ADEL

Global DRO — Global Drug Reference Online

An international reference, run by partner national anti-doping agencies, to check whether a medicine or substance is prohibited in or out of competition. It covers medicines registered in participating countries; for medicines sold in Kuwait, use KADA's medicine checker.

Open Global DRO

About ADAMS

ADAMS (WADA's Anti-Doping Administration & Management System) is WADA's global database, accessed directly on WADA's platform. The KADA website and app are not integrated with it.

Awareness

What compliance means for you.

Compliance is not only the agency's job. Athletes, coaches, doctors and federations each have obligations under the Code.

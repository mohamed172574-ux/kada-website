Board of Directors

Appointed for a four-year term, the board sets strategy, approves the budget and the national testing strategy, and safeguards the agency's independence.

Composition · Article 4 of Law No. 82 of 2018

Fifteen members

A chairman appointed by decree, the Director General and Deputy Director General, and one representative of each of twelve government bodies, at the rank of department director or above. The board elects its vice-chair at its first meeting. Names below are placeholders for presentation.

Governance

Governance committees

Under Article 5 of the law, the board forms independent committees that handle cases, exemption requests and appeals; the board does not intervene in individual decisions.

Therapeutic Use Exemption Committee

Physicians who assess requests to use a prohibited medicine for a documented medical condition.

Results Management Committee

Reviews adverse analytical findings and decides whether a violation is asserted.

Disciplinary Committee

Hears contested cases and imposes sanctions where a violation is established.

Appeals Committee

Hears appeals against decisions of the Disciplinary Committee.

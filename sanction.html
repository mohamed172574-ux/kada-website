What this ineligibility means

  • The athlete may not participate in any capacity in any competition or activity organised by a federation, club or other signatory — other than authorised anti-doping education programmes (Code Art. 10.14.1).
  • The athlete remains subject to testing throughout the period.
  • Return to training with a team or use of club facilities is permitted only in the final two months, from 10/12/2028 (Art. 10.14.2).
  • Any participation during the period results in a new period of ineligibility of equal length (Art. 10.14.3).

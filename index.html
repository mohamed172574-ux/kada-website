About KADA

Your national partner for clean, fair and healthy sport.

The Kuwait Anti-Doping Agency is the independent public authority responsible for testing, education, results management and compliance with the World Anti-Doping Code in the State of Kuwait.

  • Testing plans, sample collection and chain of custody to the laboratory
  • Results management, Results/Disciplinary/TUE committees and public register
  • Anti-doping education programme and accredited training
  • Integrated with Kuwait Mobile ID, Sahel, K-Net and DocuSign

The journey of a sample

From selection to final decision. Your rights protected at every step.

Testing follows the same procedure worldwide under the World Anti-Doping Code and WADA's International Standards. Here is what happens to your sample, and what you are entitled to at each stage.

  1. 01SelectionBased on a risk-assessed test distribution plan, plus random selection
  2. 02NotificationYou are notified without advance notice and remain chaperoned; you may bring a representative and, if available, an interpreter
  3. 03Sample collectionYou choose the equipment, split the sample into A and B bottles and seal them
  4. 04LaboratoryAnalysed under a code number in a WADA-accredited laboratory, without your name
  5. 05ResultsThe right to request B-sample analysis, attend its opening, and a fair hearing
  6. 06PublicationFinal decisions stay published for one month or the ineligibility period, whichever is longer; not mandatory for minors

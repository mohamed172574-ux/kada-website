Testing & procedure

Any athlete under the jurisdiction of a Kuwaiti federation can be tested at any time and place, in or out of competition, normally without advance notice.

You are notified by a KADA inspector, chaperoned to the control station, provide a urine and/or blood sample under observation, split it into A and B bottles yourself, and sign the digital Doping Control Form. You may bring a representative.

Refusing or evading a test is itself a violation (Art. 2.3) and can lead to a four-year period of ineligibility. You may request a short delay for legitimate reasons (medal ceremony, media, medical care) while being chaperoned.

Medicines & supplements

Use the medicine checker, which maps Kuwaiti brand names to the WADA Prohibited List, or ask your team doctor. When in doubt, don't take it until confirmed.

Apply for a Therapeutic Use Exemption (TUE) with your physician before use. The TUE Committee normally decides within 21 days of a complete application. Emergency treatment can be followed by a retroactive application. Apply online.

No supplement can be guaranteed. Strict liability means you are responsible for anything found in your sample. Use only batch-tested products and keep receipts and lot numbers.

Speak Up

Yes, if you choose. No account or name is needed, IP addresses are not logged and file metadata is removed automatically. You may instead choose confidential contact and share an email visible only to investigators.

Save the reference code shown when you submit. Enter it in the tracking section of the Speak Up page to see the status, read investigators' questions and reply anonymously. KADA cannot recover a lost code.

The Legal & Compliance investigations unit triages your report within 2 working days, then assesses and investigates it — this may lead to target testing. Where a possible anti-doping rule violation is found, the case is referred to the Results Management department.

Personal data requests

Anyone whose personal data KADA holds — athletes, athlete support personnel, course participants, suppliers and others. You can ask for a copy, a correction, deletion where the law allows, or an objection or restriction. Use the Data Access Request form.

Sign in with Kuwait Mobile ID, or upload a copy of your Civil ID, which is deleted after verification. This ensures your data is disclosed only to you.

The Data Protection Officer acknowledges your request within 2 working days and responds within 30 days. Test results and sanctions cannot be deleted on request; they are kept for the periods set by the ISPPPI.

Tenders & suppliers

Complete Supplier Registration with your commercial licence, Chamber of Commerce certificate, authorised signatory letter and bank letter. The authorised signatory verifies with Kuwait Mobile ID, and approval takes up to three working days.

Once approved, open the tender on the Tenders portal and pay the document fee with K-Net. The files unlock instantly in your account and the payment receipt is saved in your supplier dashboard.

Ask questions from your supplier dashboard before the clarification deadline. Answers are published to all document purchasers, and any addendum is emailed to them and must be acknowledged before bidding.

After evaluation by the tender committee, the award notice is published on the Tenders portal and the tender is marked as awarded. Bidders see the outcome in their supplier dashboard.

Training & events

Choose a course in the Training & Events Portal, then sign in with Kuwait Mobile ID to pre-fill your details or register as a guest. Confirmation arrives by email and SMS with a QR ticket.

Join the waiting list. When a seat is released you are notified by SMS and email and have 24 hours to accept it.

Many courses are free. Where a fee applies, it is shown on the course and paid online with K-Net when you register.

Attendance is recorded by QR check-in. Certificates for certified courses are issued to your KADA account and shared with your federation. WADA ADEL certificates can be uploaded to your account for recognition.

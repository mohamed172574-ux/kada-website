    Results management workflow

    From laboratory report to final decision — tracked, timed and audited.

    The internal engine the public never sees. Each case moves through swim-lanes with automatic deadline tracking, document locking and a full audit trail.

    Business rule enforced by the system

    All official notifications, decisions and communications with athletes are issued exclusively by the Results Department. Committee members review, deliberate and decide inside the platform; the system technically prevents them from messaging athletes, and every outgoing letter is logged.

    Content, identity & security

    Everything the agency publishes, managed in one bilingual CMS.

    Bilingual CMS

    Side-by-side Arabic / English editing, drafts and approval workflow, scheduled publishing, media library, versioning and rollback. Every page of this site is editable by KADA staff.

    Identity & RBAC

    Kuwait Mobile ID for the public, single sign-on with multi-factor authentication for staff, role-based access control with least privilege, session policies and device binding for the app.

    Audit & compliance

    Immutable audit log of every read and write on case data, document hashing, legal hold, data-retention schedules and ISO 27001-aligned controls; alignment with applicable Kuwaiti data-protection regulations.

    Notifications hub

    Templated, bilingual letters and alerts by push, email and SMS — with delivery receipts stored on the case file.

    Integrations

    K-Net payments, Kuwait Civil ID services (PACI) and Mobile ID, Sahel government platform, DocuSign, the agency's document archive, email / SMS / push notifications, WhatsApp (where approved), GIS & maps, social media, analytics and SEO. ADAMS and LIMS are not integrated — the platform links out to them — unless separately approved.

    Back-office consoles

    Tender management dashboard and procurement administration · Speak Up case & internal investigation management · Data Access Request workflow · training catalogue, capacity and attendance management · sanctions-registry publishing with automatic archiving.

    Delivery & environments

    Development → Testing (UAT) → Staging → Production with automated build and release pipelines, version control, rollback and disaster-recovery procedures; penetration testing before go-live; full QA, UAT across all roles, training and knowledge transfer.

    Secure by design

    ISO/IEC 27001-aligned controls, OWASP Top 10 and OWASP Mobile guidelines, secure SDLC, HTTPS/TLS everywhere, Content Security Policy and input validation, WAF and DDoS protection, private networking, encryption at rest and in transit, secrets held in a managed secure vault, penetration testing before go-live and continuous vulnerability scanning.

    AI platform

    Five assistants, one governed knowledge base.

    Answers are drawn only from KADA-approved rules, decisions and publications. Every answer cites its source, is bilingual, and is advisory — never a decision.

    Anti-doping education, WADA guidance, athlete rights and responsibilities, testing procedures, TUE guidance, WADA ID assistance, training and event discovery, FAQs, digital-form assistance and service referrals. Try the demo.

    Arabic/English speech-to-text, automatic translation, AI-assisted report generation, DCF guidance, intelligent document completion, voice-note transcription, context-aware recommendations and offline processing where applicable.

    Guided flows for Right-of-Access requests, Speak Up anonymous reporting and complaints: document guidance, form validation, submission tracking, privacy and compliance information.

    Semantic content search, service recommendations, training and event discovery, tender guidance, sanctions-registry assistance, WADA resource navigation, document search and frequently used services.

    Plain-language business questions over executive dashboard data: KPI analysis, compliance monitoring, trend analysis, performance summaries, predictive recommendations, automated executive reporting and decision support.

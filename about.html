Our mandate

Independent. Accountable. Aligned with the World Anti-Doping Code.

The Kuwait Anti-Doping Agency (KADA) is the National Anti-Doping Organization of the State of Kuwait. It operates independently of sports federations and the Olympic movement, with its own board and a mandate defined by national legislation and the World Anti-Doping Code.

KADA plans and conducts in- and out-of-competition testing and manages results, while its independent Results, Disciplinary and TUE committees decide on cases and exemption requests. It also maintains the public sanctions registry and runs the national anti-doping education programme.

"Every athlete in Kuwait has the right to compete on a level playing field — and the responsibility to keep it that way."

What we do

Six core responsibilities.

Testing & doping control

Annual test distribution plans, in- and out-of-competition sample collection and chain of custody to WADA-accredited laboratories.

Results management

Case registration, athlete notification, B-sample procedures and referral to the Results and Disciplinary committees within strict procedural deadlines.

Education & Awareness

Awareness campaigns, accredited courses and outreach to athletes, coaches, doctors, federations and schools.

WADA compliance

Implementation of the Code and International Standards, Code Compliance Questionnaire reporting and liaison with WADA and regional anti-doping bodies.

Intelligence & investigations

Confidential Speak Up reporting, intelligence-led testing and investigation of non-analytical violations.

Public transparency

A public sanctions registry, published decisions, annual reports and open research and publications.

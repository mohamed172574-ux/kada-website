Your data. Your rights.

Request a copy of the personal data KADA holds about you, ask for a correction, object to processing or request deletion where the law allows. We respond within 30 days, in line with WADA's International Standard for the Protection of Privacy and Personal Information (ISPPPI).

Submit a request

Identity verification protects your data from being disclosed to someone else. Sign in with Kuwait Mobile ID, or upload a copy of your Civil ID.

What this type means

KADA sends you a copy of your personal data: identity and contact details, sport and federation, doping-control forms, lab results, TUE decisions, education records and any sanctions. This is not an appeal against a test result or a decision.

Use this if a recorded detail is wrong — for example your name, date of birth, phone number or club. In the details box, write the current value and the correct one.

Ask KADA to erase personal data it no longer has a legal reason to keep, such as old contact details or a past course registration. Test results and sanctions cannot be deleted here; KADA must retain them for the periods set by ISPPPI.

Ask KADA not to use your data for a specific purpose, or to freeze it while it is reviewed — for example stopping education emails, or pausing a record you believe is inaccurate. This is not an objection to an anti-doping rule violation or a sanction.

About the conference

The National Clean Sport Conference brings together everyone with a stake in fair play in Kuwait: federations, clubs, athletes, physicians, pharmacists, educators and law-enforcement partners.

This year's edition focuses on how data, intelligence and AI are changing anti-doping — from risk-based test planning to athlete-facing assistants — while keeping human decision-making at the centre.

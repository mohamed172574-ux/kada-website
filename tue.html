Need a prohibited medicine for treatment?

A Therapeutic Use Exemption (TUE) allows you to use a prohibited substance or method to treat a genuine medical condition. Apply before you use it — online with Kuwait Mobile ID or in the KADA app. KADA's TUE Committee decides, usually within 21 days of receiving a complete application.

How the decision is made

A TUE is granted only if all four conditions are met.

01

A diagnosed condition

Your medical condition is diagnosed and supported by clinical evidence — examinations, laboratory results or imaging.

02

A necessary treatment

The prohibited substance or method is an indicated treatment, and there is no reasonable permitted alternative.

03

No extra performance

The treatment returns you to your normal state of health — and gives no advantage beyond it.

04

Not caused by doping

Your need for the treatment is not the result of earlier use of a prohibited substance or method without a TUE.

Who decides? KADA's TUE Committee: at least three physicians experienced in sports medicine, chaired by a physician, each bound by a conflict-of-interest and confidentiality declaration. Neither the athlete nor their own physician decides. Source: WADA International Standard for TUEs 2027, Articles 4.2 and 5.3.

Frequently asked

About TUEs

National-level athletes and other athletes under KADA's jurisdiction apply to KADA. International-level athletes apply to their international federation. If you already hold a KADA TUE and become an international-level athlete, your federation must recognise it.

Get the treatment you need first. Then apply for a retroactive TUE as soon as possible, with the emergency medical records. The same four conditions still apply.

It is valid at national level, including if another country's anti-doping agency tests you. For international events it must be recognised by your international federation or the event organiser — KADA will guide you through that.

You receive a written decision that explains the reasons. You can appeal to the national appeal body set out in KADA's anti-doping rules.

Under WADA rules, the costs of preparing the application — including any additional tests the committee requests — are the athlete's responsibility.

Contact KADA before changing the dose, frequency, route or duration; KADA will tell you whether a new application is needed. To continue treatment after your TUE expires, apply well before the expiry date.
